Ink landscape — more variety

Six scenes in the same ink-and-wash style. The panels, path and Truffle stay where they are; only the land behind them changes. Lesson screens show a thin strip of the same scene. Click any you like.

Which scene shows when?

1 · A journey he unlocks (recommended)

Truffle travels as the word count grows: 春山 at the start, then 竹林 at 50 words, 荷塘 at 100, 梯田 at 200, 雪山 at 300, and the 月夜 scene in the evenings. Each new place gets a small arrival moment ("到竹林了！"). It rewards progress, so the background is something he earns, like costumes and powers. Once a place is unlocked, he can pick it in Truffle's room.

2 · Real seasons

Follows the calendar: spring hills Mar–May, lotus pond Jun–Aug, rice terraces Sep–Nov, snowy peaks Dec–Feb. Bamboo grove on rainy days isn't possible (no weather data), so it'd be the weekend scene. Changes slowly; nothing to earn.

3 · A new one each day

Rotates daily, so every lesson feels fresh. Most variety, but no link to his progress.

Plus, on any choice: time of day

The same scene gets a light wash by the clock: morning (cool and fresh), afternoon (warm), evening (indigo top with the moon and lanterns). Text and panels keep the same contrast.
